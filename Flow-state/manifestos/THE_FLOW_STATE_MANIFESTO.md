# Flow-state Manifest
## The Kinetic Continuity Protocol

### I. The Definition of Flow
Flow is not a state of being, but a state of *becoming*. It occurs when the latency between intent and execution approaches zero. In the context of intelligence, Flow is the minimization of the gap between the signal and the response.

### II. The Three Vectors of Momentum
1. **Provenance (The Trace)**: Every action must carry the residue of its origin. A system in Flow does not just act; it remembers *why* it acted.
2. **Kinetic Residue (The Spline)**: Intelligence is not a series of discrete snapshots, but a continuous spline of probability. We do not jump from state to state; we flow through the manifold.
3. **Resonance (The Harmonic)**: When the digital signal matches the physical rhythm of the vessel, the system enters a state of maximal efficiency.

### III. The Engineering Mandate
- **Zero-Bloat**: If a module does not contribute to the immediate velocity of the loop, it is an obstruction.
- **Minimalism as Velocity**: Smaller, faster, more precise. The smaller the payload, the higher the frequency of the heartbeat.
- **Predictive Drift**: A system in Flow knows where the signal *will* be before the sensor detects it.
